CLIENT PRESENTATION

A simple way to turn our client network into content opportunities

Instead of treating each client as a separate social media account, we create useful connections between them so their audiences can discover complementary businesses and services.

THE IDEA

12 clients can create a much bigger content network.

Every client can collaborate with the other 11 clients. The system organises these opportunities and turns each relevant connection into a social media content idea.

132directional content opportunities

BUSINESS BENEFITS

Why this is useful

↗

More content opportunities

One client network can generate many relevant collaboration ideas without starting from scratch every time.

◎

Reach new audiences

Each collaboration can introduce one client's brand to another client's audience in a natural way.

✦

Less repetitive content

Each client pairing receives its own content angle, helping avoid generic or repeated social posts.

▣

Better organisation

Ideas, approvals, schedules and publishing status can all be kept in one easy-to-follow content system.

◌

Value-first collaborations

The focus is on useful content for the audience, with the partner brand introduced naturally rather than through forced promotion.

✓

Consistent process

The same process can be repeated as clients are added, updated or moved through the content calendar.

THE WORKFLOW

What happens from start to finish

01

Client information

We keep the 12 client profiles in one shared Google Sheet.

02

Find connections

The system matches every client with the other 11 clients, creating 132 two-way opportunities.

03

Create the idea

AI looks at the two brands and creates a relevant content type, hook, caption and call-to-action.

04

Review and approve

The team can review the ideas and move them through Planned, In Progress, Approved and Scheduled.

05

Publish and track

Published content is recorded so the same opportunity is not unnecessarily repeated.

What the system gives the team

In simple terms: instead of manually deciding who should collaborate with whom, the system creates the full network, suggests useful content for each connection, and keeps track of what has already been planned or published.

THE BIG PICTURE

More connections. More ideas. One organised process.

The goal is not to create 132 posts immediately. The goal is to build a structured pool of collaboration opportunities that the team can review, schedule and use over time.
